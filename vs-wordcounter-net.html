freewordcounter.app vs WordCounter.net: Free Alternative

WordCounter.net is one of the most well-known word counting tools on the internet, and for good reason. It has been around for years, offers a wide range of text analysis features, and has built a strong reputation among writers and students. However, WordCounter.net comes with ads, requires an internet connection, and sends your text to its servers for processing. freewordcounter.app is a simpler, privacy-first alternative that runs entirely in your browser with no ads, no login, and no server-side processing.

What does WordCounter.net do well?

WordCounter.net deserves credit for being a reliable, full-featured word counter that has served millions of users. Its keyword density tool is genuinely useful for SEO professionals who need to see how often specific terms appear as a percentage of total content. The readability scoring, which includes Flesch-Kincaid and other indices, helps writers gauge the complexity of their prose. WordCounter.net also offers a browser extension and has a clean enough layout for a tool that relies on advertising revenue.

Why choose freewordcounter.app instead?

freewordcounter.app is the better choice for users who value speed, privacy, and a clean interface. The entire application loads as a single HTML file under 50KB, making it significantly faster than WordCounter.net. There are no display ads, no pop-ups, and no cookie consent banners. Your text never leaves your browser — everything is processed locally using JavaScript and stored in localStorage. freewordcounter.app also works fully offline after the first visit, which is useful for users who write on planes, trains, or in locations with unreliable internet.

freewordcounter.app shows character count both with and without spaces as two separate, equally prominent statistics. This matters when writing for platforms like Twitter/X (280 characters including spaces), SMS (160 characters), or meta descriptions (155 characters). The dark mode toggle, font size controls, and copy stats feature make freewordcounter.app more comfortable for extended writing sessions.

Who should stick with WordCounter.net?

If you rely on keyword density analysis or readability scoring for your work, WordCounter.net is the more feature-complete option today. SEO professionals who need detailed keyword percentage breakdowns will find WordCounter.net more suited to that specific workflow. freewordcounter.app currently focuses on core counting and timing features, though readability scoring is planned for a future update.

Does freewordcounter.app have the same features as WordCounter.net?

freewordcounter.app covers the core features most users need: word count, character count with and without spaces, sentence count, paragraph count, reading time, speaking time, and word frequency analysis. WordCounter.net offers additional features like keyword density and readability scores.

Can I use freewordcounter.app offline unlike WordCounter.net?

Yes. freewordcounter.app is a Progressive Web App that works fully offline after the first visit. WordCounter.net requires an active internet connection.

Is my text private on freewordcounter.app?

Yes. freewordcounter.app stores text only in your browser's localStorage. Nothing is ever sent to a server. WordCounter.net processes text server-side for some of its advanced features.
