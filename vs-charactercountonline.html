What does CharacterCountOnline.com do well?

CharacterCountOnline.com has earned its position by being reliable and simple. The tool loads quickly, provides an immediate character count, and has been a go-to resource for users checking Twitter/X character limits, SMS length, and other platform-specific constraints. The character breakdown feature, which shows counts of letters, digits, spaces, and special characters separately, is useful for users who need granular character-type analysis. CharacterCountOnline.com also displays common platform limits (Twitter, Facebook, Instagram) alongside the count, making it easy to see at a glance whether your text fits.

Why choose freewordcounter.app for character counting?

freewordcounter.app displays character count with spaces and character count without spaces as two of its three most prominent statistics, right alongside word count. Both character count variants are visible at all times — there is no need to toggle between them or look for a secondary display. This is especially useful when writing for multiple platforms simultaneously, where Twitter/X counts characters with spaces (280 limit) and some systems count characters without spaces.

Beyond character counting, freewordcounter.app provides a complete text analysis dashboard including sentence count, paragraph count, reading time at 200 words per minute, speaking time at 130 words per minute, average word and sentence length, and a top 10 most-used words list. All of this runs in a single ad-free interface that processes text entirely in the browser. freewordcounter.app never sends text to a server, making it suitable for confidential or sensitive content.

freewordcounter.app also saves your text automatically in localStorage, supports dark mode, offers font size adjustment, and works fully offline as a Progressive Web App. These quality-of-life features make it a more comfortable tool for extended writing sessions compared to CharacterCountOnline.com.

Who should stick with CharacterCountOnline.com?

Users who specifically need a character-by-character type breakdown — how many letters, how many digits, how many punctuation marks — will find CharacterCountOnline.com more specialized for that task. For general-purpose character counting combined with word counting and text analysis, freewordcounter.app is the more versatile choice.
