What is a good word count and character count for different types of writing?

Different formats have different length requirements, and both word count and character count matter depending on the platform. Tweets and X posts are limited to 280 characters including spaces, which typically translates to 40 to 50 words. SMS messages have a 160-character limit per segment. Meta descriptions for SEO should be 150 to 155 characters to avoid truncation in search results.

Professional emails typically range from 50 to 200 words for maximum effectiveness. Blog posts perform best at 1,000 to 2,500 words for SEO purposes, with long-form content of 2,000+ words tending to rank higher in search results. College essays vary from 500 to 5,000 words depending on the assignment, with common formats being the 500-word short essay and the 2,500-word research paper. Novels generally run 70,000 to 100,000 words, with genre fiction sometimes running shorter and literary fiction occasionally running longer.
